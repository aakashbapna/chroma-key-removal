# Data provenance

`products/manifest.json` records each downloaded image's source URL, product ID, category, SHA-256, and fixed source-level train/validation/test assignment. Source: https://dummyjson.com/docs/products (sample ecommerce catalog). One photo per product is downloaded, preserving original alpha. Transparent margins are cropped and the longest edge is limited to 640 pixels. Byte-identical duplicates are removed. Images remain third-party assets; this manifest does not grant additional image rights.

Products are split BEFORE compositing, JPEG compression, or augmentation. Product IDs and exact file hashes do not cross splits; semantic near-duplicates may still occur in the catalog. Alpha channels are source-provided, not hand-corrected.

Generated imagery is kept separately. `generated/requests.json` records generation attempts without credentials. `generated/manifest.json`, when available, records prompts, model, usage, split and paths. A green background generated by an image model does not provide independent ground-truth alpha. Such data are limited to low-weight, high-confidence pseudo-label training and a separate visual-only test. They never enter quantitative alpha benchmarks as if they had exact masks.

Training composites: product scaling, rotation, placement, optional sale text, brightness, occasional transparency, pure green RGB(0,255,0), then JPEG quality 55–100 with 4:4:4 or 4:2:0 subsampling. Validation and test draw distinct variants of distinct source products.
